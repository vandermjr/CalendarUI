Calendar / Mini Calendar

Mini Calendar

The compact area used for date navigation and selection.

What does it do?

The Mini Calendar allows the user to locate a date or select a date range. The selection defines the period requested for visualization.

Week Row

A Week Row is a row of days inside the Mini Calendar. It represents one week in the compact navigation structure.

Example
Mini Calendar ┌────┬────┬────┬────┬────┬────┬────┐ │ 16 │ 17 │ 18 │ 19 │ 20 │ 21 │ 22 │ ← Week Row └────┴────┴────┴────┴────┴────┴────┘

Day

A Day represents an individual date inside the Mini Calendar.

NoteA day being present in the visual structure does not by itself mean that it belongs to the selected range.
